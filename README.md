## 🗂️ Component Architecture & Sections

1. **Header & Navbar** (`Navbar.jsx`): Frosted cyber-glass sticky navigation, mobile menu drawer, dual CTAs ("Talk to Our Team" / "Get a Free Demo").
2. **Hero Section** (`Hero.jsx`): "Transforming Ideas into Immersive Experiences", AR/VR/AI/IoT badge, live metrics, and interactive 3D headset illustration.
3. **About InGage** (`About.jsx`): "Who We Are", interactive tabs for Mission and Vision statements.
4. **Why InGage?** (`WhyInGage.jsx`): 6-card grid (500+ Projects, Immersive Technology Expertise, Cross-Platform Solutions, Creative + Technology, Industry-Focused, Customized Experiences).
5. **Technology Solutions** (`Solutions.jsx`): 8 Core Technology Pillars (Augmented Reality, Virtual Reality, Mixed Reality, 3D Visualization, Digital Twins, IoT, AI, Industry 4.0) with application breakdowns.
6. **Enterprise Solutions** (`Enterprise.jsx`): Employee Training, Industrial Safety, Product Visualization, Maintenance Training, Digital Experience Centers, Prototyping.
7. **InGage EduTech** (`EduTech.jsx`): 8 Training Categories, Academic Partnerships (Engineering, Polytechnic, Arts & Science), and sample 60-hour / 2-credit hybrid courses (IoT & Industrial Metaverse).
8. **Industries** (`Industries.jsx`): 10-industry matrix + deep dive spotlights on Manufacturing, Healthcare, Construction, Industrial Safety, Maintenance, and Experience Centers.
9. **Featured Projects** (`Projects.jsx`): Category-filtered case studies with business challenge, solution, and measurable results.
10. **Tech Stack & Creative Team** (`TechStack.jsx`): Interactive technology badge cloud and "Where Creativity Meets Technology" multidisciplinary team matrix.
11. **Statistics** (`Stats.jsx`): 500+ Projects, 51-200 Team, 2012 Founded, Pan-India Presence.
12. **Careers** (`Careers.jsx`): Open roles across 3D design, Unity/Unreal, AR/VR, AI, IoT, and embedded firmware.
13. **Contact & HQ** (`Contact.jsx`): Interactive enquiry form with interest selector & Perungudi Chennai headquarters details.
14. **Footer** (`Footer.jsx`): 4-column sitemap, technology links, EduTech training tracks, contact details, and copyright.
